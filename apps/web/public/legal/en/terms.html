Eligibility and age

Kyalulu Core is free AGPL-3.0 OSS. Local inference and BYOK require no Kyalulu subscription, cloud account or K-Credits. Users pay their BYOK provider and follow its terms.

Cloud requires Google/email-link authentication, a verified email and agreement to terms including eligibility, privacy and disclosed destinations. You must be at least 14 years old. Users under 18 need a parent or guardian’s consent. The service is offered worldwide, but where local law prohibits or restricts use, that law prevails. Age and region are self-declared at login and are not documentary identity, age or residence verification (adult features require the separate age verification below).

Content policy

The operated cloud has two routes. The OpenRouter route is for general audiences and also follows each model’s and provider’s policies. The route running the operator’s own model on rented GPUs offers adult conversations, including sexual and violent content, only to users who have enabled adult features (not yet available).

Adult features can be enabled only by accounts confirmed as 18 or older by a third-party age-verification provider. Self-declaration does not enable them. Without verification, you cannot chat with adult characters. Adult characters may be shown to signed-out visitors with non-explicit images and descriptions.

Authors may publish a character as adult only after agreeing to the operator’s adult content provider conditions and passing the operator’s prior review (automated screening and review by the operator). The operator may hide or remove characters that violate these terms at any time after publication.

On every route, the following are prohibited: sexual content involving anyone under 18 or depicted as under 18, including fictional characters; sexual content involving real people; sexual exploitation; incitement to hatred; and concrete assistance with serious illegal harm, including step-by-step instructions for real-world crimes. On violation, the operator may stop conversations, remove characters or suspend accounts. Automated input and output checks will be introduced before adult features launch. No external safety screening is performed today.
