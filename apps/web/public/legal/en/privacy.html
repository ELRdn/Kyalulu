Privacy policy (pre-publication draft)

Unapproved draft. Current access is a one-owner private test. General registration, sales, ads, new image storage/sync and public Community are unavailable. Legal acceptance for the general beta, including operator facts, retention and analytics implementation, is incomplete.

Device and operated cloud

Local Core stores conversations, settings and memories on your device. Local-only inference does not send text to the operated cloud. BYOK and optional sync send necessary data through the enabled route. Remote is a separate relay route.

Cloud authentication uses Supabase Google/email links, a verified email, agreement to eligibility within the terms and consent to the destination disclosure. Accounts, conversations, characters, Persona, World, memories, sync revisions and credit ledgers are separated by owner. Cloud storage is not encryption readable only by your device: the operated Runtime processes content for generation and storage. Authentication tokens and BYOK keys are encrypted at rest on the server.

Generation sends necessary conversation history, character/Persona/World settings and memories to OpenRouter and the selected provider. Inputs, outputs and saved works receive no external safety screening today and are not sent to a separate screening API. Automated input and output checks will be introduced before adult features launch. Adult conversations are checked by a classifier model running on the GPUs described below and are not sent to an external screening API. New image storage/sync is disabled.

Conversations using adult features (not yet available) are sent not to OpenRouter but to the operator’s own model running on rented GPUs. The GPU provider is planned to be RunPod, with processing in the United States.

Age verification for adult features (not yet available)

When you enable adult features, a third-party age-verification provider (planned: Yoti) checks whether you are 18 or older. Facial age estimation runs first; an ID document check is requested only when the result is borderline. Face and ID images are processed by the provider; Kyalulu does not receive or store them. Kyalulu stores only the result (whether you are 18 or older), the method, the time and the provider’s reference ID, received directly from the provider by our server, and keeps them while your account exists. The provider’s retention period and processing region will be named before launch.

Six provider-specific routes

All use OpenRouter with a pinned destination and no automatic fallback. Standard uses DeepSeek V4.1 Flash through InferenceNet. The following describes the current public status and request policies; it is not independent verification of every provider’s contracts or actual retention.

  • DeepSeek V4.1 Flash / Baidu (baidu/fp8): permits retention of inputs/results and AI training/improvement. Each send requires separate explicit consent starting unchecked, without carryover after cancellation, expiry or an owner change. Retention periods, processing locations and OpenRouter-specific agreements remain unconfirmed.
  • DeepSeek V4.1 Flash / Decart (decart/fp4): requests denial of training-purpose data collection. Actual retention, processing locations and contractual terms remain pending.
  • DeepSeek V4.1 Flash / InferenceNet (inference-net/fp8): requests denial of training-purpose data collection. Actual retention, processing locations and contractual terms remain pending.
  • GLM 5.3 Flash / InferenceNet (inference-net/fp4): requests denial of training-purpose data collection. Actual retention, processing locations and contractual terms remain pending.
  • GPT-6 Luna Flex / OpenAI (openai/flex): requests denial of training-purpose data collection. The public-status disclosure describes monitoring logs containing inputs/results normally retained for up to 30 days, with possible legal/safety extensions. Actual application through OpenRouter and processing locations remain pending. Flex latency and availability vary.
  • Claude Haiku 5.5 / Google Vertex (google-vertex/global): requests denial of training-purpose data collection and ZDR. Actual global processing locations, retention and contracts remain pending. Requesting ZDR does not guarantee zero operator retention or zero retention across the entire route.

Baidu training consent is separate from Kyalulu history storage and analytics consent. A policy denying training does not imply zero operator or provider retention. Data may be transferred/stored outside your region. Actual conditions must be confirmed for OpenRouter, each provider, Supabase, Google authentication and separate BYOK/Remote routes. OpenCode Go/Muse Contributor are not part of current initial availability. Destination changes require updated disclosure and renewed consent where necessary.

Optional analytics

If GA4 is used, its scope is only the official site (kyalulu.com) and operated cloud (cloud.kyalulu.com). In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, analytics defaults off and starts only after explicit consent. Elsewhere it is on by default after agreement to the terms. In every region, refusal or withdrawal stops analytics sends. Local Core, Desktop, CLI and Remote do not receive GA4 integration. Acceptance of actual activation, consent controls and settings remains pending.

Measurement includes screen/event categories, language and approved canonical URLs on the official website. Cloud page URLs contain only the origin (scheme and host), without individual conversation or character paths, queries or fragments. Referrers contain only their origin; UTM values use an explicit fixed allowlist. Conversations, memories, character content, card/owner/session IDs, email, authentication tokens, API keys and free text are excluded. Direct traffic is not inferred as AI referrals.

Connections to Google may process network information such as IP addresses and, after consent, analytics cookies/browser identifiers; anonymity is not guaranteed. The GA4 property, retention, withdrawal controls and suppression of default automatic collection must be confirmed before final disclosure. Conversation, training or required authentication consent does not replace analytics consent. Advertising is currently disabled.

Terms and analytics preferences

The analytics default depends on your region as described above. It is optional: disable it or withdraw through terms and privacy settings at any time. Google tags do not load before consent. Disabling analytics does not prevent login or chat. Residence declarations are not sent to Google Analytics. Analytics is separate from content checks.

Deletion, retention and contact (pending)

Sync defaults off and excludes API keys, Remote keys, device settings, experiment artifacts and imported originals. Export/deletion features do not establish completed deletion from operator systems, providers or backups. Planned retention: conversations are kept until you delete your account and are removed from backups within 30 days after deletion; operational logs are kept for 90 days; age-verification results while your account exists; payment records for 7 years as required by law. The operator’s legal name, address, responsible person and contact; access/correction/closure/deletion procedures and deadlines; retention for authentication and ledgers; and international-transfer/third-party conditions remain pending. A plan’s restore window is not a deletion deadline. Payments are planned through a payment processor that accepts adult content; its name and handling of payment data will be finalized before sales open.
