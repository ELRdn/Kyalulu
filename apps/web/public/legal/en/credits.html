K-Credits terms (pre-publication draft)

Unpublished, unapproved draft. Cloud registration and sales remain disabled until seller information and applicable conditions are confirmed.

The retail reference is 10,000 K-Credits per $1. Top-ups: $5 for 50,000 and $10 for 100,000. Plus costs $5/month with 12,500 monthly credits; Pro costs $10/month with 25,000. Free accounts receive a one-time 1,000 trial only if verification and global funding conditions are satisfied.

Generation charges are variable integers. Aggregate input, output, safety screening and retries at six times API cost, then round up once. Successful generation costs at least 1; failed generation costs the user 0. The maximum is shown and reserved before submission; unused reservation is returned on settlement. Admission assumes a cache miss and up to three attempts.

Initial operator-route settlement uses the usage.cost reported by OpenRouter. Published token price ceilings are used only for maximum reservations, not as substitutes for missing actual cost. A generation with missing actual cost fails with zero user charge; uncertain costs remain in the operator budget. Paid generation and new sales remain disabled until real API, billed-cost and sales-condition acceptance.

Monthly credits expire at the contract period end; purchased credits at 180 days; trial/ad credits at 30 days. Earliest expiry is spent first. Credits cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. BYOK, sync, storage, ordinary editing/deletion/export cost 0 credits. New generation may pause when the operator budget is exhausted.

Refunds and chargebacks revoke associated balances; investigation may suspend service. Treatment of unused credits on service closure and any applicable prepaid-instrument requirements must be confirmed before publication.
