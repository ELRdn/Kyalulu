Generation and destinations

Ordinary generation starts directly on send, with the destination and maximum credit charge shown in chat. This candidate offers only DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash through OpenRouter/InferenceNet. The other four routes are unavailable. Standard uses DeepSeek V4.1 Flash through InferenceNet. There is no automatic fallback to a different model/provider or automatic resubmission.

The Baidu route permits retention of conversations, settings, memories and generated results, and their use for AI training/improvement. Each send requires separate explicit consent starting unchecked; general login consent is insufficient. Consent is not carried over after cancellation, expiry or an owner change. Retention differs on other routes as well; see the privacy policy.

Users must have the necessary rights to supplied characters and content. AI can be wrong; perfect personality, memory, speed and a fixed number of conversations are not guaranteed. Generation may stop for upstream failures, unknown costs or exhausted funding.
