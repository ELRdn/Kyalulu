K-Credits terms (pre-publication draft)

Unapproved draft. Current access is a one-owner private test; sales, ad rewards and general registration are disabled. The intended ten-person free beta and trial grants await legal, operational and funding acceptance.

Trial and price reference

The nominal retail reference is 10,000 K-Credits per $1. The planned free-beta trial is a one-time 1,000 Credits per person (nominal $0.10), valid for 30 days, subject to verified authentication, previous-grant checks and global funding. Automatic trials are 0 in the current private test. Credits are not provider cost and do not guarantee a conversation count.

Unavailable sales candidates are $5/50,000 and $10/100,000 top-ups, Plus at $5/month with 12,500 monthly Credits, and Pro at $10/month with 25,000. These are not sales availability or promised recurring grants; sales regions, refunds and prepaid-instrument requirements remain pending.

Settlement on success

Charges are integers per generation: aggregate API costs for input, output and attempts within generation, apply a sixfold markup, convert to Credits and round up. Successful generation costs at least 1; failure costs the user 0. There are currently no external SFW-screening calls or charges. The selected destination and maximum charge appear in chat. User Credits are not reserved; successful generation settles within the shown ceiling. Estimates conservatively allow a cache miss and up to three attempts within the ceiling, without automatic resubmission of failed sends or provider fallback.

OpenRouter routes settle using reported usage.cost. Token reference prices determine admission/ceilings and do not replace missing actual bills. Missing/invalid costs or overruns stop generation; zero user charges on failure do not erase conservatively retained operator costs and audit records. Removal of user Credit reservation is separate from operator funding holds. Retrieving the same completed generation costs no additional Credits.

Expiry, suspension and pending conditions

Designed expiry is 30 days for trial/ad Credits, 180 days for purchased Credits and contract period end for monthly grants; earliest expiry is spent first. Purchases, ad rewards and monthly grants are currently unavailable. Credits cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash. BYOK, sync, storage and ordinary editing/deletion/export cost 0 Credits; BYOK provider charges are separate.

A balance does not guarantee generation: exhausted operator funds, bill reconciliation or upstream failures may suspend it. Free-beta provider funding is separate from VPS/backup operating costs. No automatic monthly subsidy activation or reset of private-test funding/unknown costs is performed.

The operator/contact, sales regions, refunds/chargebacks, unused balances on closure, statutory retention/deletion periods and regional prepaid-instrument requirements remain pending. Final sales conditions and legal approval flags require separate acceptance.
